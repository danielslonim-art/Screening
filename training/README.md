# Training content

Training material for Global Screening analysts. Each deck lives in its own dated folder, with a
`REVIEW.md` that records the review, the sources checked, and the sign-off still needed.

## Content standard (applies to every slide)

Every piece of information shown to analysts must answer three questions:

1. **In short:** what are we telling you, in very simple terms?
2. **Why it matters:** why should you care?
3. **What it means for you:** what you do with it in *our* process. Name the step or the tool: the
   alert queue, the 4-eye check, L2 escalation, the RFI template, the ownership tools, TM / EDR referral.

If a fact cannot answer questions 2 and 3, it goes in the speaker notes or a facilitator appendix, not on
the slide.

## Also required

- **Tone.** Measured and explanatory, in complete sentences. Use a clear structure with a theme for each
  section and a hand-over between sections.
- **Motion.** Use click-to-reveal only where the audience votes or predicts first: cases, exercises,
  "what's the answer here?" moments. Otherwise keep motion to a subtle fade. Never animate the key
  message.
- **Sources.** Cite a source for every regulatory statement (CLAUDE.md §2). Cite Checkout procedures by
  document and section.
- **Data.** Use only anonymised or fictional cases. Never name a real customer or merchant (CLAUDE.md §4).
- **Sign-off.** The Sanctions Officer signs off before delivery. AI-drafted material is a draft until
  then (CLAUDE.md §1).
